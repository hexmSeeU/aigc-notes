The official `Caveat[wght].ttf` was downloaded on 2026-10-12, instantiated at
weight 600, and subset with FontTools to the letters used by the learning-map
heading and English annotations. The WOFF is self-hosted, so reading the blog
does not request a font from Google or another third party. Chinese labels
retain the site's system-font stack. The license does not reserve a font name.
